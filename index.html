Portal mission · direction 07

Fill, with two orbits.

Two hairline rings sit behind the mark, each carrying a single dot on its own line, turning in opposite directions at different speeds. Because they pass behind the blades, the dots disappear and reappear — the orbit reads as depth rather than decoration.

Counter-rotation is the whole trick. Same direction reads as one thick ring; opposite directions at a 1 : 1.5 ratio never repeat the same arrangement, so the eye keeps finding it new.

The rings use non-scaling-stroke. Without it a 1.5-unit stroke in a 460-unit viewBox becomes 0.08 px at 24 px and vanishes. With it the ring stays a hairline on screen at every size.

Honest limit. At 24 px the dots are under a pixel and the rings are decoration you can barely see. It still reads, but if the Portal uses 24 px spinners, ship the core-only file there and this one for 40 px and up. Two files, one identity — a normal thing for an icon system to do.
